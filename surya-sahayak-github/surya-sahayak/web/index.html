How the estimate is made

Three steps, all shown in your report. The language model only reads your bill and answers questions; the arithmetic is ordinary code you can check.

  1. Read the billAmazon Bedrock reads the units, sanctioned load, electricity company and PIN code. If anything is unclear, we ask you instead of guessing.
  2. Replay the weatherFive years of hourly sunshine and temperature for your spot (Open-Meteo, ERA5) show what a south-facing roof would have produced, checked against PVGIS's long-term average.
  3. Apply today's rules and pricesCentral and state subsidies, typical installed prices, your tariff and a 25-year cash flow give the savings and the payback range.
What we useUnits, sanctioned load, electricity company, and the PIN code or city. Your approximate location, if you allow it.
What stays outYour name and account number aren't used in the calculations, and weather or map services never receive them.
What to confirmSubsidy amounts, prices and payback are estimates. Confirm them on pmsuryaghar.gov.in and with a registered vendor.

Estimates, not a sanction or a guaranteed return. Subsidy rules change: confirm on pmsuryaghar.gov.in and solar.delhi.gov.in.

Your bill is read with Amazon Bedrock. Weather and map services only see an approximate location (about 1 km), or your PIN code or city, never your name, bill number or street address. With no GPS and no address on the bill, your network address is used once to guess the city.
